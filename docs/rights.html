01

Overview of Data Subject Rights

Under the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL), GDPR, and the Huawei AppGallery User Privacy Guidelines, you enjoy statutory control over your personal data. Signal Keeper fully recognizes and supports:

🔍

Right to Access & Copy

Know what is stored

You have the right to confirm whether personal data relating to you is being processed and obtain a copy of such data.

🗑️

Right to Erasure (Deletion)

Permanent data wipe

You have the right to request deletion of all your game progress, settings, and cached advertising identifiers.

↩️

Right to Withdraw Consent

Revoke permissions anytime

You may withdraw consent for advertising processing at any time by declining in-app prompts or resetting device OAID.

🔒

Right to Deregister (Cancel)

Account-free autonomy

Signal Keeper maintains no cloud accounts. Clearing local storage achieves 100% complete account-equivalent deregistration.

02

Self-Service Guide: Deleting Game Data

Because Signal Keeper stores all puzzle progress (stars, moves, solved rounds) strictly in your device's isolated local storage, you can immediately and permanently delete all your data at any time without waiting for developer intervention:

1

Open Device Settings

Navigate to your device's Settings → Apps & Services (or Application Manager).

2

Select Signal Keeper

Locate and select Signal Keeper in the installed application list.

3

Clear Data & Cache

Tap Storage → tap Clear Data and Clear Cache. Alternatively, uninstalling the app permanently purges all local game data.

03

How to Withdraw Consent & Reset Advertising ID (OAID)

When optional rewarded ads are requested, Huawei Petal Ads uses the Open Advertising ID (OAID) provided by the device operating system. You retain full control over this identifier.

For Huawei / Honor Devices (HarmonyOS & EMUI):

  1. Open Settings → tap Privacy (or Security & Privacy).
  2. Tap Ads (or Ads and Privacy).
  3. Tap Reset Open Advertising ID (OAID) to immediately generate a fresh, unlinked identifier.
  4. Optionally enable Disable Personalized Ads to prevent ad targeting across all applications.

For Standard Android Devices:

  1. Open Settings → tap Google (or Privacy).
  2. Tap Ads → tap Reset Advertising ID or Delete Advertising ID.
ℹ️
In-Game Consent Control: In Signal Keeper, before any ad is loaded, you are presented with an optional-ad disclosure dialogue. Tapping Cancel immediately cancels the request without initializing the Huawei ad engine. Restarting rounds is always 100% free!
04

Account Deregistration / Cancellation Policy

Huawei AppGallery Review Guidelines (§7.2) require apps with account mechanisms to provide an easy account deregistration channel.

✅
Accountless Architecture Statement: Signal Keeper does NOT implement any user account system, registration flow, or cloud login credentials. Because no account records or profiles exist on remote servers, user account cancellation is inherently fulfilled. By uninstalling the application or clearing the application data from device settings, all associated game state is immediately, permanently, and irrevocably deleted.

Supervisory Authority & Dispute Resolution

If you believe our processing of your personal information infringes upon your statutory rights, or if you are dissatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local regulatory authority:

  • China: Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) / Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) 12321 Reporting Center (www.12321.cn).
  • European Union: Your local National Data Protection Authority (DPA) under the GDPR.
  • Other Regions: The consumer protection or privacy commissioner of your jurisdiction.

We encourage you to contact us first at privacy@hovahdigitalsolutions.com so we can address your concerns immediately.
