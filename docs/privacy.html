Welcome to Signal Keeper (hereinafter referred to as "the Game" or "the Application", Android package: com.hovahdigitalsolutions.signalkeeper, Huawei App ID: 119227547), operated and published by Hovah Digital Solutions (hereinafter "we", "us", or "our").

We deeply respect and prioritize the protection of your personal information and privacy. In compliance with applicable laws and regulations—including the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China (PIPL), the Cybersecurity Law of the People's Republic of China, the Data Security Law of the People's Republic of China, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the Huawei AppGallery Review Guidelines (Section 7: User Privacy)—we have established this Privacy Policy to transparently disclose how we process personal data and how you can exercise your statutory rights.

💡
Key Principle: Signal Keeper is an offline-first puzzle game designed around data minimization. We do not require registration or login accounts, do not collect personal profiles, and store your gameplay progression entirely on your local device.
02

Personal Information We Collect and How We Use It

We adhere strictly to the principles of legality, fairness, necessity, and good faith. We only collect the minimum personal data strictly necessary to fulfill game functionality:

A. Local Game Data (Non-Personal Progression)

When you play Signal Keeper, the game records your puzzle state, remaining moves, round restarts, and earned stars (up to 30 stars across 10 rounds). This data is stored strictly in your device's isolated local app directory (user:// in Godot). It is never synchronized to remote cloud servers or linked to your real-world identity.

B. Optional Rewarded Video Advertising Data

In Signal Keeper, players may voluntarily choose to watch a rewarded video advertisement to undo a rotation and refund a move. No advertisement is loaded or requested upon launching the game. Only when you explicitly tap "Watch Ad" and confirm the in-app disclosure, the Huawei Petal Ads SDK is initialized to retrieve and display the advertisement.

To facilitate ad rendering, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and reward verification, the Huawei Petal Ads SDK processes:

  • Device Identifiers: Open Advertising ID (OAID), carrier code, device model, operating system version.
  • Network Information: IP address, network connection type (Wi-Fi, cellular), signal strength.
  • Ad Interaction Records: Ad request times, impressions, clicks, video playback progress, and completion events.
🛡️
Non-Personalized Ads Notice: Signal Keeper explicitly configures Petal Ads requests with setNonPersonalizedAd(1). The game does not build personal interest profiles and does NOT request or access your fine or coarse GPS geographic location.

C. What We NEVER Collect

We explicitly affirm that Signal Keeper does NOT collect, read, or process:

  • Real names, phone numbers, email addresses, identity numbers, or passwords.
  • Precise or coarse GPS location data.
  • Contact lists, SMS messages, call logs, camera, or microphone audio.
  • Payment card credentials, banking information, or financial records.

Operating System Permissions & Usage

To guarantee full transparency, the following table lists all Android operating system permissions declared and utilized by Signal Keeper:

Permission Type Purpose & Scenario Is Optional?
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Required by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK to load and display optional rewarded video ads when the player requests an undo. Yes (Offline gameplay is 100% playable with free round restarts without internet).
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Used to check if an active network connection exists before attempting to request an ad, preventing unnecessary battery and data waste. Yes (Checked dynamically).
⚠️
No Sensitive / Dangerous Permissions: Signal Keeper does not declare or request any Dangerous Permissions (such as READ_PHONE_STATE, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, READ_CONTACTS, CAMERA, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

05

Data Storage, Retention, and Security

A. Storage Location & Retention Duration

All gameplay saves and configurations remain strictly stored within the sandbox of your personal device. Data is retained until you manually clear application data via system settings or uninstall the game. Data processed by Huawei Petal Ads is retained in accordance with Huawei's official retention schedules and applicable legal requirements.

B. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security safeguards. All ad communications with Huawei servers are encrypted via HTTPS / TLS protocols to prevent interception, tampering, or eavesdropping.

C. Cross-Border Data Transfers

We do not operate backend servers that transfer user data across international borders. Any network transmission initiated by the Huawei Petal Ads SDK adheres to Huawei's regional compliance mechanisms and certified cloud infrastructure (such as Huawei Cloud regional data centers).

Your Data Subject Rights

In accordance with personal information protection laws and Huawei AppGallery guidelines, you have comprehensive rights over your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Know: You have the right to know what personal data is processed.
  • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): You can completely erase all game data by clearing the app cache or uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may decline the ad disclosure in-app, or disable personalized ads / reset your OAID in device settings.
  • Right to Account Deregistration: Signal Keeper operates account-free. Deleting local data is functionally equivalent to complete account deletion.
